What it is

One app, every Polymarket workflow.

A Quasaria-themed build of the open-source Krypt PolyBot desktop app. Every feature of the original is kept; the look is the only thing that changed.

🤖

AI agents over MCP

Connect Claude, Cursor, Codex or any MCP client as a named trader, or let Autopilot run one on a schedule. Every agent is fenced in by caps, a daily loss stop and a forecast it must beat, then scored against the market.

📈

Trading terminal

Discover, search and trade any Polymarket market by hand: live order book, price history, tape, resolution-rule checks and paper or live tickets.

🐋

Whale & momentum scanners

Watch the public trade feed for large-wallet activity and short-term momentum clusters, with confidence and edge scores on every signal.

🪞

Copy trading

Mirror wallets you choose, with sizing, entry-price limits, a concurrency cap and a loss limit.

⚖️

Perps market maker

A quoting engine for Polymarket Perps with its own link, caps and a flatten-everything switch.

🧪

Strategy scripts & backtests

Write your own Python strategies, run them in shadow mode against the live market, backtest on recorded data and arm them yourself when you are satisfied.

⏱️

15-minute crypto engine

Rules-based engine for the short-term crypto Up/Down markets, with its own sizing, windows and collection of data to backtest.

🏛️

Agent Hub

A live 3D view of what your agents and engines are doing, plus cockpit, positions, history and logs.

Safe by default

Nothing trades until you say so.

The defaults are built so that opening the app cannot cost you money.

  • Paper-firstThe app starts in Paper: real order books, imaginary money, no wallet needed. Going Live is a deliberate checklist.
  • Engines off by defaultMain, crypto, copy trading, perps and scripts each ship switched off, each with its own switch.
  • Local-first, no telemetryTrade history and settings live on your computer. No account, no analytics, no usage stats sent back. Settings lists every outbound host.
  • Keys stay on your machineA wallet key is only used to sign orders locally and is stored encrypted where the OS supports it. AI agents can never read it.

Honest risk notice.

Trading can lose real money. This is not financial advice.

  • Live mode uses real money. Polymarket trades on Polygon with real USDC and there is no demo exchange. In Live, a switched-on engine places real orders and you can lose some or all of what is in the wallet, quickly, including while you are away. Use a dedicated wallet funded only with money you can afford to lose.
  • The strategies are unproven. The bundled strategies are heuristics with no proven, fee-adjusted edge. Scores, "edge" figures and AI forecasts are illustrative, not promises. An AI agent can be wrong with total confidence.
  • Not financial, investment, legal or tax advice. You are responsible for following Polymarket's terms and the laws where you live.
  • No warranty. The software is provided as is and may contain bugs that cause wrong, missed or unwanted orders.
About the portal. The portal runs entirely in your browser on demo data. It has no backend, no wallet and no connection to Polymarket, and nothing in it can place a trade. Markets, books, prices and signals are synthetic. The real app is a desktop application that you run yourself.
